# Licences & Approvals

## Primary Pathway: Qatar Financial Centre (QFC)

**Target Licence:** Category 2 (Arranging Deals in Investments)
**Timeline:** Q2 2026 (June target)
**Strategic Rationale:**
* Web Summit Qatar 2026 incentive package ($0 fees for 4 years; saves ~$125K).
* Gateway to GCC market (Qatar, UAE, KSA family offices).
* 12-16 week approval timeline (faster than UAE ADGM).
* Lower regulatory capital ($250K vs $750K for ADGM).
* Family office-optimized regulatory framework.

**Key Requirements:**
* QFC entity incorporation (February 2026).
* Regulatory capital: $250,000 USD.
* Qatar-based compliance officer.
* Physical office in Qatar (QFC Tower or Qatar Science & Technology Park).
* AML/KYC policies and procedures.

**Positioning:** Non-discretionary execution tool (NOT robo-advisor).
* Users define all investment parameters (allocation, risk limits, rebalancing thresholds).
* ReFi.Trading executes WITHIN user constraints.
* Comparable to TradingView, MetaTrader, IBKR API (execution-only).
* Category 2 appropriate (Category 3 would require discretionary authority).

## Secondary Pathway: UAE ADGM Category 3A

**Target Licence:** Category 3A (Dealing in Investments as Agent)
**Timeline:** Q4 2026 (December target)
**Strategic Rationale:**
* Local UAE presence for deeper institutional penetration.
* Enables retail expansion in 2027 (UAE has ~1M retail traders vs Qatar's ~50K).
* Regulatory sandbox (RegLab) for testing retail offering.
* Crypto-friendly framework (Virtual Asset Framework).

**Key Requirements:**
* ADGM entity incorporation (Q3 2026).
* Regulatory capital: $750,000 USD.
* ADGM-based compliance officer (can be dual-appointed with Qatar).
* Physical or serviced office in Abu Dhabi.
* Annual supervision fee: ~$15,000 (ongoing).

**Phase 1 (2026):** Serve UAE institutional clients from Qatar QFC entity.
**Phase 2 (2027):** ADGM licence for local retail expansion.

## Key Regulatory Advantages

**Non-Custodial Architecture:**
* Assets remain at client's existing broker (Interactive Brokers, Saxo Bank).
* No Category 4 (Custody) licence required.
* 60-70% lower regulatory capital vs custodial competitors.
* Simplified compliance (no client money rules/CASS).

**Cryptographic Compliance (Dual-Proof Gate):**
* zk-VaR proofs verify trades comply with client risk limits BEFORE execution.
* Chainlink ACE on-chain policy verification.
* Immutable audit trail on zkSync Era L2 blockchain.
* Real-time compliance (prevents violations vs detecting post-trade).

**Patent Protection:**
* 1 USPTO patent pending + 5 drafted.
* Covers Dual-Proof Gate architecture, RL portfolio optimization.
* Estimated $5-10M IP value.
* 12-18 month competitive moat.

# Financial Model Summary

The financial model projects three years of revenue, costs, and cash flow for ReFi.Trading, incorporating our **dual-market strategy** targeting institutional clients (GCC family offices and lean funds) in 2026, followed by retail expansion in 2027.

---

## Key Financial Outputs

* **Total Projected Value (NPV):** US $611M
* **Internal Rate of Return (IRR):** Approximately 21%
* **Return on Investment (ROI):** Around 194%

---

## Revenue Model Overview

### Phase 1 (2026): Institutional-Only (GCC Market)

**Target Market:** Qatar, UAE, and Saudi Arabia family offices and lean funds.
**Pricing:** $2,000/month flat subscription (no execution fees).

| Market | Conservative | Target | Stretch |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Qatar** | 15 clients = $360K ARR | 25 clients = $600K ARR | 35 clients = $840K ARR |
| **UAE** | 20 clients = $480K ARR | 40 clients = $960K ARR | 60 clients = $1.44M ARR |
| **KSA** | 10 clients = $240K ARR | 20 clients = $480K ARR | 30 clients = $720K ARR |
| **Total 2026** | **45 clients = $1.08M ARR** | **85 clients = $2.04M ARR** | **125 clients = $3.00M ARR** |

* **2026 Actual Revenue (7 months live):** $540K (conservative) to $1.5M (stretch).
* **Launch Trigger:** Qatar QFC licence approval (June 2026 target).
* **Client Acquisition:**
    * Web Summit Qatar (Feb 2026): 50+ family office meetings → 5-10 pilot clients.
    * GCC expansion: UAE institutional clients (Q3 2026), KSA institutional clients (Q4 2026).
    * Conversion rate: 20% of qualified leads → signed clients.

### Phase 2 (2027): Retail + Institutional

**Retail Tiers:**
* **Basic:** $99/month
* **Pro:** $299/month
* **Enterprise:** $999/month

**Projected 2027 Revenue:**
* **GCC Institutional:** $2.04M ARR (85 clients @ $2K/month).
* **Global Retail:** $7.05M ARR (2,500 users blended).
* **Total 2027:** $9.09M ARR.

**Launch Trigger:** UAE ADGM retail licence (Q2 2027 target).

### Phase 3 (2028): Marketplace + Tokenization

**Additional Revenue Streams:**
* Strategy marketplace: 30% take rate on third-party strategies.
* Token economics: $REFI utility token for execution fee rebates.
* Performance fees: Optional for fund clients.

**Projected 2028 Revenue:** $55M ARR.

## Cost Structure

**Major Cost Categories:**

1.  **Cloud Infrastructure:** AWS/token-subsidized DePIN (40% cost reduction).
2.  **Team Salaries:** Engineering, compliance, operations, growth.
3.  **Compliance & Licensing:**
    * Qatar QFC: $250K regulatory capital + $0 annual fees (4 years).
    * UAE ADGM: $750K regulatory capital + $15K annual fees.
4.  **Marketing & Growth:** Influencer partnerships, content, community.
5.  **Overhead:** Legal, accounting, office space.

**Total Operating Costs (2026):** ~$849K
**Break-Even:** Month 32 (target scenario)

---

## Key Assumptions

### Revenue Assumptions

**Institutional (2026):**
* **Average client value:** $24,000/year ($2,000/month).
* **Launch:** June 2026 (Qatar QFC licensed).
* **Ramp:** 7 months live trading in 2026.
* **Churn:** <5% annually (sticky institutional relationships).
* **Client acquisition:** Web Summit Qatar + GCC business development.

**Retail (2027+):**
* **Conversion rate:** 60% of Weekly Active Safe Paper Traders (WASPT) convert to paid.
* **Average Revenue Per User (ARPU):** $120/month (blended across tiers).
* **Monthly churn:** <3% target (power-user base).
* **Alpha cohort:** 150+ signups → 40 WASPT by Q1 2026.

### Cost Assumptions

* **Regulatory capital:** $250K (Qatar QFC) + $750K (UAE ADGM) = $1M total.
* **Team scaling:** 3 FTE (2026) → 8 FTE (2027) → 15 FTE (2028).
* **Infrastructure:** Token-subsidized DePIN reduces cloud costs by 40% vs AWS/GCP.
* **Non-dilutive capital:** $300-350K (grants, tax credits, cloud credits).

## Scenario Analysis

The model uses three scenarios to stress-test projections:

### Conservative Scenario
* **2026:** 45 institutional clients = $1.08M ARR (7 months live = $540K revenue).
* **2027:** 65 institutional + 1,500 retail = $5.5M ARR.
* **2028:** 100 institutional + 10K retail = $25M ARR.
* **Metrics:** NPV: $350M | IRR: 16%.

### Base (Target) Scenario
* **2026:** 85 institutional clients = $2.04M ARR (7 months live = $1.02M revenue).
* **2027:** 165 institutional + 2,500 retail = $9.09M ARR.
* **2028:** 200 institutional + 30K retail = $55M ARR.
* **Metrics:** NPV: $611M | IRR: 21% | ROI: 194%.

### Aggressive (Stretch) Scenario
* **2026:** 125 institutional clients = $3.00M ARR (7 months live = $1.5M revenue).
* **2027:** 250 institutional + 5,000 retail = $15M ARR.
* **2028:** 350 institutional + 50K retail = $85M ARR.
* **Metrics:** NPV: $950M | IRR: 28%.

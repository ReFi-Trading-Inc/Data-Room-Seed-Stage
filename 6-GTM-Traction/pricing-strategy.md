# Pricing Strategy

ReFi.Trading employs a **dual-market pricing strategy** targeting both retail traders and institutional clients (family offices and lean funds), with distinct pricing models optimized for each segment.

---

## Market Segmentation

### Retail Market (Phase 1: 2026-2027)
Active retail traders with $10K-$500K portfolios seeking institutional-grade automation.

### Institutional Market (Phase 1: 2026 - GCC Focus)
Family offices and lean funds in GCC region (Qatar, UAE, Saudi Arabia) with $50M-$500M AUM.

---

### 2. Institutional Pricing (GCC Family Offices & Lean Funds)

**Target Market:** Qatar, UAE, Saudi Arabia family offices and lean funds ($50M-$500M AUM).

**Pricing Model:** Flat monthly subscription (NO execution fees).

| Client Type | Monthly Fee | Features | Strategic Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Family Offices** | **$2,000/month** | • Portfolio rebalancing automation<br>• Non-discretionary execution<br>• Cryptographic risk verification (zk-VaR)<br>• Multi-asset support<br>• Dedicated account manager<br>• Quarterly strategy reviews<br>• White-label option | Predictable cost structure; aligns with long-term retention over transaction volume |
| **Lean Funds** | **$2,000/month** | • Same as family offices<br>• Multi-portfolio management<br>• Investor reporting tools<br>• Compliance audit trails | Infrastructure-as-a-service for emerging managers |

**Key Positioning:**
* **Non-discretionary execution tool** (NOT robo-advisor).
* Users define all parameters: allocation, risk limits, rebalancing thresholds.
* ReFi.Trading executes WITHIN user constraints.
* Comparable to TradingView, MetaTrader, IBKR API (execution-only).

**Why Flat Subscription (No Execution Fees)?**
1.  **Predictability:** Family offices prefer fixed costs over variable execution fees.
2.  **Alignment:** Incentivizes long-term client retention, not excessive trading.
3.  **Simplicity:** Easier compliance and accounting vs. basis-point fees.
4.  **Competitive:** Distinguishes from traditional fund administrators charging AUM %.

**Note on Retail Transition (Phase 2: 2027+):**
Once regulated for retail (UAE ADGM 2027), may introduce hybrid model: subscription + transaction fees.
* *Example:* $50-$150/month subscription + 10 bps execution fee (70% rebated to brokers).
* *Until then:* Institutional-only pricing remains subscription-based.

## Pricing Strategy Rationale

### For Retail:
1.  **Tiered Model:** Captures willingness-to-pay across user segments.
2.  **Low Entry Barrier:** $99/month accessible to serious retail traders ($25K+ portfolios).
3.  **Upgrade Path:** Natural progression as users scale portfolios and sophistication.
4.  **Competitive Positioning:**
    * Below traditional robo-advisors (0.25-0.50% AUM = $250-$500/year on $100K).
    * Above DIY solutions (free but requires technical expertise).
    * Transparent vs. black-box bots (3Commas, Cryptohopper).

### For Institutional (GCC):
1.  **Flat Fee Alignment:** Predictable cost structure preferred by family offices.
2.  **Premium Positioning:** $24K/year positions as institutional-grade service.
3.  **No AUM %:** Unlike traditional advisors (1-2% AUM), we charge fixed fee regardless of portfolio size.
4.  **Scalability:** $2K/month sustainable for $50M+ AUM clients; high margin for ReFi.Trading.

## Pricing Flexibility & Future Phases

### Phase 1 (2026): Institutional-Only
* **Pricing:** $2,000/month flat subscription.
